⚠️ Math Literacy — Past papers, consistently

Your teacher said you have the ability and the attitude — the key is consistency. Work through past papers and class exercises regularly, mark them immediately, and understand every wrong answer before moving on. Keep a formula reference card handy and review it before each paper.

🍅 Study in short bursts

Work for 45–50 minutes, then a proper 15-minute break. Walk around, get water, stretch. Your brain consolidates memory during breaks — they are part of studying, not a reward for finishing.

🌙 Sleep is non-negotiable

Memory is formed during sleep. Aim for 8 hours every night. Staying up late to cram the night before an exam almost always does more harm than good — your rested brain will recall what your tired one forgets.

📝 On exam day: read the whole paper first

Spend the first 5–10 minutes reading through the full paper before writing anything. This lets you plan time, spot easier questions to tackle first, and reduce anxiety by knowing what's ahead.

💙 When motivation disappears

Start with the smallest, easiest task on the list. The hardest part is starting. Once you've done one thing, the next feels possible. You don't need to feel motivated to begin — begin, and the motivation often follows.
